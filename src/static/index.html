Interview Talking Points & Architecture

Key engineering decisions & design principles for Day 13

1. Cold Start Strategy

Pure collaborative filtering fails when a new user arrives (empty rating vector) or new items are ingested. AuraMatch employs an empirical Bayesian prior: WR = (v / (v + m)) * R + (m / (v + m)) * C combined with immediate projection of in-session feedback (likes/star ratings) into the latent SVD factor space u_session = R_session * V^T.

2. Diversity vs. Accuracy & MMR Re-Ranking

Greedy top-K recommenders suffer from severe redundancy (e.g., 5 superhero movies or 5 tracks by the same artist). We implement Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR):
MMR = argmax [ λ * Rel(d) - (1 - λ) * max Sim(d, s) ]. By exposing the λ slider, users directly balance tight mood relevance against serendipitous genre exploration.

3. Offline Evaluation Metrics (NDCG, MAP, ILD)

Recommendation systems cannot rely on accuracy or RMSE alone because position matters:
• NDCG@K evaluates whether highly relevant items appear at the very top of the ranking with logarithmic position discounting.
• MAP@K quantifies precision across rank cutoffs.
• Intra-List Diversity (ILD) measures the mean pairwise distance between recommended items to mathematically penalize homogeneity.

4. Emotion Extraction & Mood Trajectories

When a user states: "exhausted but want something uplifting", a naive sentiment classifier sees "exhausted" (negative/low energy). Our clause segmentation identifies contrastive conjunctions, separating the user's current fatigue from their desired regulatory target (Valence: 0.88, Energy: 0.75).
